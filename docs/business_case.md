# Business case

## Context

A fictional Solo Queue analytics team wants to understand performance and engagement patterns in a competitive gaming product.

## Core questions

1. Which factors are most strongly associated with match wins?
2. How does performance vary by tier, role, region and play style?
3. Which champions are both effective and consistently played?
4. What player segments exist from a behavioral perspective?
5. Which signals could inform coaching, matchmaking UX or engagement initiatives?

## Stakeholders

- Product Manager
- Game/Competitive Designer
- Community / Operations Lead
- BI / Analytics Lead

## Decision framing

For every major finding, complete this template:

**Finding**  
What happened?

**Evidence**  
Metric + comparison + sample size + time period.

**Interpretation**  
What could plausibly explain it?

**Action**  
What should the team test or monitor?

**Measurement**  
Which KPI determines whether the action worked?

**Caveat**  
What can the data not prove?

## Minimum final findings

- One performance driver
- One player-segment insight
- One champion/role insight
- One engagement insight
- One data-quality limitation
